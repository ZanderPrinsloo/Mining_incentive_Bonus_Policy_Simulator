What this tool does

Enter aggregate production & labour figures for a period (no per-crew detail needed), turn on the bonus parameters that apply, set their weights, and see the projected bonus payout. Nine section templates are pre-loaded from the real Harmony policy documents as starting points — duplicate one and adjust rates to model a proposed change before it's implemented.
