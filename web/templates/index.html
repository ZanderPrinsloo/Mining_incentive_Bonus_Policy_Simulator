What this tool does

Enter aggregate production & labour figures for a period (no per-crew detail needed), turn on the bonus parameters that apply, set their weights, and see the projected bonus payout. Nine section templates are pre-loaded from the real Harmony policy documents as starting points — duplicate one and adjust rates to model a proposed change before it's implemented.

Achievement Bands

Distribute crews across payout/achievement tiers instead of entering per-crew detail. Payout % is a direct multiplier on that band's Amount (e.g. "this tier is only paid at 90% of rate"). Achievement % scales the m²-per-employee assumption for that band against the section's own average (100% = matches it) — only affects the m² basis, routed through Efficiency so Rate stays R/m² instead of R/crew; has no effect under the R/Man basis. Ignored if Achievement Bands is off. "Add Remaining" adds the leftover crews (Crew Count minus every other band's) as a new 0%-payout band, with Achievement % solved so every band's Total m² adds up to Total m² Achieved — so you can see how many crews didn't earn the bonus. Whichever band has the lowest Payout % is then kept as that running remainder automatically: editing any other band's Crew Count or Achievement % re-solves it so the totals never exceed Manual Inputs.
